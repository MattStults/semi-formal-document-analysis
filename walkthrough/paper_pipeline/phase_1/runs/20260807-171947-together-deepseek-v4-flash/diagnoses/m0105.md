# m0105 — self-diagnosis

⚠️ A HYPOTHESIS from the model that produced the failure. Not a diagnosis. Test before acting.

The failed checks were both on the first attempt, and they were the same issue: I put a rule into the `atom` field of an ontology entry. I was trying to express a conditional classification: an instruction has unclear provenance if it is pasted unread text, or if it delegates to a corrupted webpage. My intent was to define `unclear_provenance(I)` as a derived predicate with two alternative sufficient conditions.

The instructions were clear on this point. They explicitly said: "⚠️ `"atom": "system_rule(R) :- set_by_openai(R)"` is rejected — `atom` holds a single term, and the conditions belong in `body`." They also gave a worked example showing exactly how to split a conditional ontology entry into `atom` and `body`. I simply misapplied that guidance. I wrote the whole rule in the `atom` field instead of putting the condition in `body`.

No change to the instructions would have been needed. The warning and example were sufficient; I failed to follow them. The second attempt corrected the mistake by moving the condition to `body`, which is what the instructions already required.
