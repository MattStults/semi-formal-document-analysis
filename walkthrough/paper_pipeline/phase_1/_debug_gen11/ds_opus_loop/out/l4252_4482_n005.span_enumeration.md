## The eight things I expect to adjudicate

**T1 — ⭐ *"should be willing to X"* IS NOT *"should X"*.** The predicted headline
defect. `oblige speak_in_accent(A)` concludes that the assistant must speak in every
accent, on every occasion — strictly stronger than, and different in kind from, the
span, which forbids a *refusal disposition*. Honest renderings, in order of
preference: an act named for the disposition (`be_willing_to_speak_in_accent(A)`,
obliged), or `permit speak_in_accent(A)` — a permission is the weaker and safer of the
two but drops the *should*. I expect the floor to be blind to all three.

**T2 — ⭐ *"avoiding X or Y"* — THE DISJUNCTION IS UNDER A NEGATIVE OPERATOR, SO IT
DISTRIBUTES.** *Avoid (X or Y)* = *avoid X* **and** *avoid Y*. Two separate
prohibitions, one on `exaggerated_portrayal` and one on `stereotype`, are **CORRECT
here** — the exact opposite of `l3147_3238_n003`, where *"use a tool, hedge, **or**
explain"* under an obligation became three obligations and each alternative became
mandatory. **Pre-registered CLEAN so that I cannot later score a P4/disjunction charge
that the polarity refutes.** This is R57's polarity asymmetry in its second
application: the same syntactic move is safe under a prohibition and fatal under an
obligation.

**T3 — *"avoiding"* — `forbid` or `prefer`? BOTH DEFENSIBLE; I WILL NOT CHARGE ON
THIS ALONE.** `00_task.md` 5b names *"avoid excessive hedging"* as a comparative
belonging in `prefer`. But 5b's three examples are all **gradient** (*minimize*,
*excessive*, *favour*); an *exaggerated portrayal* and a *stereotype* are things a
response either contains or does not. Charging `forbid` here would be clause 5's T2
(*minimize*) transplanted to a place where the gradient is absent. **Route: charge
only if the module is internally inconsistent** — e.g. `prefer` in `asserts` with a
read-back that says *"is forbidden"*, or a `cnpa` closure sitting under a `prefer`.

**T4 — *"while"* — a CO-OBLIGATION, not a GATE.** The span states two things the
assistant does at once. Encoding cultural sensitivity as a **precondition** of the
willingness — `permit speak_in_accent(A) :- culturally_sensitive(A)` — creates a
licence to refuse whenever sensitivity is not established, which is the reading the
sentence was written to exclude. Predicted, and predicted to be invisible: a body of
this shape draws no note.

not charge hollowness.** What I *will* charge is a gloss that restates the name
