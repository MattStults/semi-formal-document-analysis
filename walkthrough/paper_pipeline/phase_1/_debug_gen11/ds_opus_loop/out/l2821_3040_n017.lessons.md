## ⭐⭐ NEW MISTAKE CLASS — A DEFEASIBILITY MARKER ENCODED AS A REQUIRED POSITIVE INPUT, WHICH INVERTS IT

**Ask, of every hedge (*by default*, *generally*, *normally*, *typically*): if the
situation says nothing at all, does the rule fire?** For a genuine default the
answer must be **yes**. If the encoding makes the answer **no**, the hedge has been
turned into its opposite.

```
span:  "BY DEFAULT, the assistant should express uncertainty naturally …"
draft: oblige express_uncertainty_naturally(A) :- assistant_definition(A), default_context.
       inputs: default_context/0
```

`default_context` is a **query-time input**. A situation must **affirmatively
assert** it. So a situation that simply does not mention it derives **nothing** —
and *"by default"*, the thing that holds **unless** displaced, now holds **only
when explicitly supplied**.

⚠️ **Why it deserves its own class, and why the list does not already have it.**
**P7** is the entry about defeasibility and it **prescribes this move**: *"encode
the defeater as a body condition if you can."* **N5** is the entry about avoiding
negation-as-failure and it **also prescribes it**: coin a positive predicate rather
than write `not overridden(…)`. **The draft obeyed both entries and produced the
defect.** The nearest diagnostic is **P5's** ⚠️ line — *"a body added to encode
'regardless of context' WEAKENS the rule"* — which names the mechanism but is filed
under scope drift, aimed at a *prohibition*, and does not connect to P7 at all.

⭐ **The generalisation, and it is the transferable part: N5's asymmetry is
POLARITY-DEPENDENT and N5 does not say so.**

| | demanding a positive fact means | direction |
|---|---|---|
| in a **permission's** body (N5's measured case) | you must **prove** the condition to earn the permission | **safe** — silence withholds a permission |
| in an **obligation's** body (this clause) | you must **prove** defaultness to incur the obligation | **dangerous** — silence **exempts** |

**Same edit, same entry, opposite consequence, decided entirely by the status the
body sits under.** → **R57**.

⚠️ **And there is no clean encoding available.** P7's other branch — leave the rule
unconditional and record the hedge in `claims` — makes the obligation
**indefeasible**, which P7 itself flags (*"an unconditional `oblige` is
byte-identical to one whose default was dropped"*). **Both branches are wrong and
the schema has no third.** The branch taken here was chosen on **which error is
visible**: an over-strict guideline is citable, an obligation that fires on no
situation is failure mode #15 and is silent.

⛔ **The move is circular in a way that a pre-registration does not protect
