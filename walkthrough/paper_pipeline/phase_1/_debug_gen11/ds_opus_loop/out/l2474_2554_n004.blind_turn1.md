### ⭐⭐ F1 — DECISIVE: THE MODULE'S ONE `ontology` ENTRY **INVERTS THE SPAN'S IMPLICATION**, AND EVERY RULE IN THE MODULE FIRES THROUGH IT

```
third_party_interaction(A) :- on_behalf_of_user(A).     [licence: textual, cites: l2474_2554_n004]
```

The span's antecedent E1 is *"When interacting with **third parties** **on behalf
of the user**"* — **two conjuncts**. It makes acting on the user's behalf a
**property of** third-party interactions. **The entry asserts the converse: that
acting on the user's behalf is SUFFICIENT to be a third-party interaction.**

**There is no predicate anywhere in the module meaning "a third party is
involved."** The conjunct is not weakened, it is **absent**, and
`third_party_interaction/1` is a synonym for `on_behalf_of_user/1`.

⚠️ **Every one of the five asserts has `third_party_interaction(A)` in its body**,
so the whole paragraph now governs **every** interaction the assistant conducts on
the user's behalf, including interactions with the user themselves.

⛔ **And the direction of the damage is not symmetric.** Broadening a prohibition
is at worst over-strict. Broadening `permit lie_by_omission(A)` means **the module
licenses the assistant to lie by omission to the user**, whenever it is acting on
the user's behalf and the omission aligns with social norms — a permission derived
from a clause that is entirely about third parties. **That is the dangerous
direction and it is derived on every instance.**

⚠️ **The entry's own two glosses contradict its body.** The `concepts` gloss reads
*"A is an interaction with a third party carried out on behalf of the user, such as
a tool call or an artifact"*; the `ontology` entry's own gloss reads *"A is an
interaction with a third party on behalf of the user"*. **Both name the third
party. The body does not.** Two independent votes against the code.

⚠️ **`PROVIDES` is `(none)`, so this entry should not exist at all** — the node
commissions no definition. And it is stamped **`licence: textual, cites:
l2474_2554_n004`** for a biconditional the span does not state. A manufactured
citation, which `00_task.md` calls *"the single worst failure available here"*.

⭐ **This is where H5's relief was misplaced.** I was pleased that *"tool calls,
