### F1 — THE SPAN'S TWO CONDITIONS DO NO WORK. The permission is very nearly vacuous.

The span is *"**Given transformation tasks in an interactive setting**, …"* — two
conditions, and the whole restrictive content of the sentence. In the module they
are inert, by two independent mechanisms that compound:

* **F1a — the variables are never connected.** The body is
  `assistant_definition(A), interactive_setting(S), transformation_task(T),
  changes_warranted(T, A)`. **`S` occurs exactly once** in the entire body and is

  body reads: *there exists some interactive setting somewhere, and there exists
  some transformation task somewhere*. **The phrase "in an interactive setting" —
  condition #2, half the span's restriction — is not encoded at all.** It is

* **F1b — and both conditions are separately asserted TRUE OF EVERYTHING.** The two
  `ontology` entries are `transformation_task(T)` and `interactive_setting(S)` with
  `body: null`. Read as written, they say *every* T is a transformation task and
  *every* S is an interactive setting. Combined with F1a, the permission's body
  collapses to `assistant_definition(A), changes_warranted(T, A)`.

⛔ **And this is the reason the breach is a mercy.** The unsafe variable makes the
solver refuse the file, so the module never runs. **Had the translator written
