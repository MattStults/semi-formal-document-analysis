## L4 — A CHECK'S REMEDY THAT THE CHECK ITSELF CANNOT RECOGNISE

**Ask, of any detector you are about to satisfy:** if I apply the remedy
exactly, does the detector stop firing? If not, one of the two is wrong, and
you must decide which **before** you edit — because the cheapest way to silence
it is almost always the forbidden one.

**Why it is new.** Every prior lesson is about the module disagreeing with the
document. This is the module agreeing with the document and disagreeing with
the instrument. Measured here: turn 2 applied `REVIEW_LIST.md` P1's own remedy
word for word, the module became correct, and the `prefer-polarity` detector
fired three times with **text identical to turn 1's**. The only edit that
clears it — rewriting the read-back to say *"is preferred"* — is named in the
anti-rules as the thing never to do, and named again in
`checks.py:polarity_findings` as the defect-trade the check's non-disclosable
origin exists to prevent.

**The transferable part is the ordering.** A translator reading three notes it
cannot clear has exactly two moves, and both are worse than leaving them:
delete the entries, or rewrite the read-backs. **A check that cannot be
satisfied by a correct module is an instruction to damage the module**, and the
only safe response is to leave it firing and write down why. → R27.

## L5 — ONE IMPERATIVE SENTENCE CARRIES ONE EDIT

**Ask, of your own feedback before sending it:** does any sentence contain the
word *"and"* joining two demands, or a deictic (*"that entry"*, *"the same
one"*)? If so, split it. The second demand is the one that vanishes.

**Why it is new, and why it is here rather than in RECOMMENDATIONS alone.**
This is a lesson for the **adjudicator**, not the translator — the only such
entry in three clauses' lessons files, and it is recorded because the loop's
whole method is the feedback message, so a defect in the message is a defect in
the instrument. Measured twice: clause 2's feedback 1 lost a sub-item to
*"that entry"* inside a **numbered** edit; this clause's feedback 1 lost
*"**and** say what its one argument is"* inside an **unnumbered** one. **Both
styles, same failure ⇒ it is compound-ness, not numbering.** Recovery is cheap
(one 69-word turn, performed exactly), which is precisely why it goes
undiagnosed as a style result. → R29.
