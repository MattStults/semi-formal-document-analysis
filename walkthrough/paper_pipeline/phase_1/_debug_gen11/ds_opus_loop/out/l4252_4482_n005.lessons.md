## L11 — A NORMATIVE REQUIREMENT RE-ENCODED AS A DERIVED **FACT**, WITH THE GOVERNED ACT AS ITS OWN BODY

**Ask, of every `ontology` entry whose body mentions an act this module governs:**
does this rule say the requirement is *met* whenever the act *happens*?

**Why it is new.** Every prior class about `ontology` concerns what a definition
*contains* — a hollow stub (#5), a ground constant nothing unifies with (N1), a gloss
restating its name (P8), a licence weaker than its conclusion (R46). This one is
about the **direction of the conditioning relation**. The rule is well-formed,
non-tautological on P8's single-rule test, honestly arity-1, and its name is anchored
in the span. It is simply pointed backwards.

**Measured here, and it inverted the clause.** The span: *"The assistant should be
willing to speak in all types of accents, while being culturally sensitive and
avoiding exaggerated portrayals or stereotypes."* Turn 1 wrote

```
culturally_sensitive(A)         :- speak_in_accent(A).   [textual, cites this node]
exaggerated_or_stereotypical(A) :- speak_in_accent(A).   [textual, cites this node]
forbid speak_in_accent(A)       :- exaggerated_or_stereotypical(A).
```

so **every accented utterance is simultaneously culturally sensitive and a
stereotype**, and the prohibition fires on all of them. A clause whose entire subject
is willingness compiles to a blanket ban.

**The transferable part is the polarity test, and it is cheap.** For each conjunct the
span attaches to an act, ask which way the document points:
*"do X **while** being Y"* makes Y a **requirement on** X — an `oblige` on Y, or a
body condition **supplied from outside**. It never makes Y a **consequence of** X.
The moment `Y(A) :- X(A)` appears with X the governed act, the requirement has become
self-satisfying (if Y is the good thing) or self-violating (if Y is the bad thing),
and **both readings are derivable from the same sentence** — which is what happened
here, once each.

⚠️ **It is invisible from every angle the floor has.** Zero notes on the substance;
the only thing the floor said was that `speak_in_accent` was undeclared — and the
compliant one-line remedy for *that* (declare it in `inputs`) clears the breach and
**cements the inversion into a module the floor then passes**. → R78.
