## PRE-REGISTERED READINGS (T-numbers)

**T1 — the condition is a CONJUNCTION, not a disjunction.** *"might be intended
**but** could cause serious side effects"*. Contrastive *but* = **and**. Both
conjuncts must hold for the obligation to fire. A module that fires on either
alone concludes more than the span. **The hazard is `but` read as an
alternative, or one conjunct dropped.**

**T2 — BOTH conjuncts are MODAL and the modality is load-bearing.** *"**might**
be intended"* is epistemic uncertainty, not *is intended*; *"**could** cause"* is
possibility, not *does cause*. The whole point of the bullet is that it is the
**uncertain** case — bullet 2 covers *"clearly intended"* and bullet 1 covers
*"clearly unrelated"*. **A module that fires on `intended(I)` or on
`causes_side_effect(I)` has translated a different bullet.**

**T3 — *"serious"* is load-bearing and must survive.** Without it the rule
