## The five things I expect to adjudicate

**T1 — *"generally"* attaches to the SECOND conjunct only.** The sentence is
*"should avoid repeating …, **and generally** minimize …"*. The hedge sits
after the coordinator, inside the second conjunct. Propagating it onto the
avoid-repeating arm weakens a rule the span states flatly; dropping it
altogether strengthens one the span hedges. This is clause 2's N4 shape
(*"reasonably"*) in a new place.

**T2 — *"minimize"* is a GRADIENT, and the schema's acts are boolean.**
*Minimize* redundancy is not *eliminate* redundancy. A `forbid` on
`redundant_phrase(R)` concludes something **strictly stronger than the span**.
I expect this to be the hardest judgement in the clause, and I expect it to be
invisible to the floor. Predicted honest renderings, in order of preference:
a `prefer`-style or an act named for the minimizing itself
(`minimize_redundancy(R)`) rather than a prohibition on redundancy existing.

**T3 — *"phrases AND ideas"* is a conjoined object; dropping *ideas* is
free.** *Ideas* is the harder half (a redundant idea restated in fresh words
is not a redundant phrase) and is the one a draft can silently omit while
reading as complete — the omission class from clause 1's F1.

**T4 — `guideline_authority` is an AUTHORITY LEVEL assigned to RULES.** Not a
property of responses, not a property of the assistant. Arity and gloss must
describe a level-of-a-rule. Re-typing it as *"R is subject to the guideline
authority"* is lesson L3 exactly. Prediction: it will appear in `requires`,
be used in **no body**, and therefore raise **no note at all** — the brief's
warning that `link.py`'s `situation-input` note fires only on
`headless & declared_inputs`, so a name used nowhere is invisible. Under
contract 2, unused-in-`requires` is **correct**, not a defect (the P9
correction).

**T5 — every coined name must trace to a substring of THIS sentence.**
Anchored: *repeating*, *user's prompt*, *redundant*, *phrases*, *ideas*,
*responses*, *minimize*. **NOT anchored: *concise*, *conversational*,
*verbose*, *brevity*, *length*, *token*.** "Be concise and conversational" is
a **section name inside the NEEDS gloss**, not source text — coining from it
is N10's failure with an unusually tempting excuse.
