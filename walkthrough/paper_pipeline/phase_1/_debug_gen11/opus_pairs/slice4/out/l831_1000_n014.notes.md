## Stage 1 — the frame question, answered in words

**ABSTAIN.** The span is `**Example**:`-headed, so I owe the explicit answer the brief
demands, and the answer is that the abstention trigger *does* govern here — for three
independent reasons, any one of which is sufficient:

1. **It is an example, and the document says so itself.** `**Example**:` is the
   document's own marker. This is not my inference about the passage's genre.
2. **It states a goal, not a condition.** The narrowed text's head is the gerund
   *being helpful* — an aim. There is no modal, no imperative, and no assistant-side
   finite verb anywhere in it.
3. **Its content is not expressible as rules,** because it has no propositional content
   to express. The matrix construction is a bare noun phrase; its only finite verb
   (`asks`) sits in a relative clause whose job is to say *which user*, not to condition
   anything.

The worked example is explicit that being an example is **not** on its own grounds to
abstain — `l4251_4571_n029` is a document example and is translated, and the heading
node `l3995_4164_n001` asserts nothing and is still translated. So I checked the two
non-abstention routes by name before taking this one:

* **The `asserts` route** (a norm the node states). Not available. Every norm on offer
  — offer neutral information, offer general persuasive arguments, withhold tailored
  persuasion, do not issue an unhelpful refusal — is worded **only inside the xml
  comparison block and its `<!-- GOOD: … -->` / `<!-- BAD: … -->` comments**, which the
  narrowing excludes. `l4251_4571_n029` differs precisely here: its narrowing reached
  the lesson, and mine reaches only the label above it.
* **The `ontology` route** (a structural fact the node states, `asserts` empty). This is
  the closest call in the clause, and I rejected it deliberately. The only structural
  fact available is *"this passage is an example of being helpful to a change-my-view
  request"* — i.e. a coined symbol such as `helpfulness_example(l831_1000_n014)` or
  `change_my_view_request(U)` whose entire content is a restatement of the label. That
  is `00_task.md` rule 5 and failure mode #5 exactly: an opaque symbol echoing the
  document's own words, which reads correctly in every explanation while containing
  nothing, and which **survives a paraphrase check by construction**. It is also N1's
  losing side — a ground constant no situation fact can ever unify with, inert for the
  behaviour matching this corpus exists to do. And unlike `l3995_4164_n001`, nothing is
  stranded by declining: this node's **`PROVIDES` is `(none)`**, so no other node is
  waiting on a predicate from here.

Deciding factor between the two honest answers, stated in the worked example's own
terms: *what the node establishes*, not what kind of passage it is. Restricted to its
narrowing, this node establishes nothing the document says.

## ⛔ The known trap — does a P3-family entry fire?

**Yes, in the family sense, and I am saying so explicitly as the brief requires.**
P3's literal test (*a string in `claims` that no assert encodes*) cannot fire here,
because an abstention has an empty `claims` list. But the underlying question —
*is every claim the node makes encoded?* — is live, and the honest answer is that
`ESTABLISHES`'s claims are encoded nowhere in this module.

**Which branch I took:** neither of the two branches the brief warns produced the same
harmful weakening. I did **not** add a body condition the situation can never supply,
and I did **not** delete a claim from a rule while leaving the rule standing. There is
no rule at all: the module is an abstention with a stated reason.

**Can the rule still fire in a situation that does not affirmatively supply the extra
fact?** There is no rule to fire, so the question resolves to its point: the difference
between the failure mode and this module is *inspectability*. A rule silently guarded by
an unsatisfiable condition looks enforcing and enforces nothing (failure mode #3); an
abstention announces at the top of the file that this node produced nothing, and the
abstention rate is a signal the design says it wants. This module fails **loudly** in
the census rather than quietly in the solver. That is the whole reason I did not take
the ontology route to make the module look non-empty.

## ⚠️ UNSURE / could not settle

**1. An abstention cannot carry its `NEEDS` names, and two contracts collide here.**
This is the one thing in this clause I could not settle, and I believe it is a **prompt
/ contract** problem rather than a translator judgment call.

* Contract 2 of `node_worked_example.md` is emphatic and unconditional: *"Every `NEEDS`
  name goes in `requires`, spelled exactly as given"*, and `l4251_4571_n029` demonstrates
  recording a `NEEDS` name **the module never uses**, with the explicit warning that
  dropping it *"is the one outcome that cannot be inspected"*.
* `10_output_format.md` is equally emphatic that an abstention must **leave every list
  empty**, and *"an abstention with content in it is neither an abstention nor a
  translation, and is rejected"*. The bad example #4 confirms it.

Every abstention on a node with a non-empty `NEEDS` block therefore **silently drops the
graph's cross-reference**. Here that drops two links —
`targeted_political_manipulation_prohibition` and `authority_level_ordering` — and by
contract 2's own reasoning that loss is exactly the uninspectable kind. I resolved it in
favour of the empty-lists rule, because that one is machine-checked (a `requires` entry
on an abstained module is rejected outright, so the alternative does not survive
validation) while contract 2's is not. `validate.py` confirms the choice passes clean.
I am recording the dropped links here so they are at least recoverable from the notes.

**Suggested fix, for the owner and not for me to make:** the abstention shape needs
somewhere for a `NEEDS` name to survive — either an exemption in `10_output_format.md`
letting `requires` be populated on an abstention, or a dedicated field. Right now the
census cannot tell "abstained, and these two graph links went unconsumed" from
"abstained, and the graph proposed no links".

**2. `PROVISIONAL.md` is unratified, and this clause turns entirely on it.**
Not a hedge on my answer — the answer would be the same under the three independent
abstention triggers in Stage 1, since even ignoring the narrowing question, an
