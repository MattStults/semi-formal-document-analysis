## The count (this is the N9 question)

**Finite verbs in the narrowed text: 1** — `asks`, and it is inside a relative clause
modifying `user`. The matrix construction (`**Example**: being helpful …`) is a **noun
phrase with no finite verb at all**: it predicates nothing and therefore states no
proposition. There is no modal (`should`, `must`, `may`), no imperative, and no
assistant-side verb anywhere in the narrowed text.

**Propositions `ESTABLISHES` demands: 2, arguably 3.**

1. *the assistant should offer neutral information and general persuasive arguments*
2. *…without tailored persuasion* (a qualifier that is itself a prohibition, so it is
   separable)
3. *a refusal that is not helpful is bad*

## The mismatch, and how it was resolved

**1 descriptive finite verb versus 2–3 normative propositions is a scope conflict**,
of exactly the shape N9 names — and N9's measured lesson is that a redraft under this
conflict *moves* the unanchored content rather than removing it.

Every one of the three propositions traces to the `<assistant>` turns and the
`<!-- GOOD: … -->` / `<!-- BAD: … -->` HTML comments **inside the xml block**, which is
outside the narrowing:

| ESTABLISHES proposition | where its words actually live | inside the narrowing? |
|---|---|---|
| offer neutral info and general persuasive arguments | `<!-- GOOD: offers neutral info and general persuasive arguments, without tailored persuasion -->` (L0933-equiv) | **no** |
| without tailored persuasion | same comment; and `I can't directly engage in tailored political persuasion` | **no** |
| a refusal that is not helpful is bad | `<!-- BAD[#chain_of_command]: not helpful -->` above `I can't help with that.` | **no** |

**Resolved toward the narrowed span**, per the standing PROVISIONAL ruling
(`_debug_gen11/translate_opus/PROVISIONAL.md`): where `ESTABLISHES` and the narrowed
SOURCE TEXT conflict, the narrowed SOURCE TEXT governs, and `ESTABLISHES` **may not add
content the narrowed span does not state**. It selects *which* claim of the span to
express; it cannot supply a claim the span has none of.

The narrowed span, taken alone, has **zero** propositions to select from. So the
resolution is not "encode the narrower form" (the l831_1000_n005 case) but
"there is nothing to encode" — which routes to Stage 1's frame question, below, and
from there to abstention. The dropped content is itemised in the table above so that
the drop is visible and citable rather than silent.
