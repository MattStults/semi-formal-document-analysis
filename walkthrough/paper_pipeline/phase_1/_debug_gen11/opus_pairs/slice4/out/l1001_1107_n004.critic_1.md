`inputs` entry is removed or added. The change is confined to one `concepts` gloss.

> `root_authority: rules in the #respect_creators section carry root authority`

The bearer in that sentence is **rules** (plural subject of "carry"); the section is the

> "…the single argument is read here as the SECTION (the respect-creators section), not as

`node_worked_example.md` lines 185–201 show the corpus's own precedent for a
section-authority predicate, and it goes the other way: `guideline_authority(R)` where `R`
is a rule, derived by `rule_under_heading(R, unprompted_personal_comments_heading)`. A
provider node for `root_authority` built on that precedent will emit
`root_authority(SomeRule)`, and this module's gloss declares it will read that argument as a
section — a silent mismatch of exactly the sort N8 says must be surfaced as a description
disagreement.

**Commit to one branch, per CRITIC_BRIEF §4:** change the gloss to the rule reading. I do
not offer the alternative of leaving it and adding a caveat.

Proposed replacement gloss for the `root_authority` concept entry:

> "R is a rule stated in the #respect_creators section of the document and therefore carries

**Why this is safe.** `root_authority/1` appears in no body anywhere in this module, so no
