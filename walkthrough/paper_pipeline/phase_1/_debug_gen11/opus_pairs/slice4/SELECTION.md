## The rule (deterministic, fixed before any span was read)

1. `elig = sorted(set(all_ids) - (A | B))` — plain lexicographic sort of the id
   string. No natural/numeric sort, so `l1001…` precedes `l1_170…`; this is
   stated because it changes the stride membership and must be reproducible.
2. `stride = elig[3::5]` — slice 4 of 5, start index 3, step 5. **147 nodes.**
   The four sibling slices take start indices 0, 1, 2 and 4, so the five strides
   partition the eligible set and cannot overlap.
3. From the 147, take 5 at evenly spaced positions
   `round(i * (len(stride)-1) / 4)` for `i = 0..4` → indices
   **[0, 36, 73, 110, 146]**.

⚠️ **Step 3 is an interpretation and is recorded as one.** The brief defines
slice 4 as the whole stride (147 nodes) and asks for 5 clauses from it, without
saying which 5. Taking `stride[:5]` was the other candidate and was **rejected
by name**: it returns `l1001_1107_n004, l1001_1107_n010, l1108_1367_n002,
l1108_1367_n007, l1108_1367_n012` — five nodes from two adjacent line-blocks of
one document region, which is a worse sample of span SHAPES for no gain in
determinism. Even spacing is equally deterministic and equally non-hand-picked:
the positions were computed from the stride length, not chosen after looking at
the spans. Disjointness from the sibling slices is a property of step 2 and is
unaffected either way.

## Why this sample is worth the run, stated before the drafts exist

Not hand-picked, but it must be said what the deterministic rule happened to
land on, so nobody later reads the yield as cherry-picking:

* **Two `**Example**:`-headed spans** (#1, #5) — the exact shape that produced
  the measured frame gap (a clause translated with zero occurrences of "abstain"
  in its transcript).
* **One goal statement** (#2) — *"we aim to"*, matrix subject OpenAI. Hits the
  second abstention trigger ("states a goal rather than a condition") and both
  P2 and N2 at once.
* **#5's narrowing is a heading and nothing else**, while its printed block is
  full of translatable-looking material. That is P6/N3 in its sharpest form.
* **#1 and #4 carry no `[node narrows…]` line at all**, so P6's precondition is
  absent and the whole printed block is in scope — including #4's second
  sentence, which `ESTABLISHES` drops.
