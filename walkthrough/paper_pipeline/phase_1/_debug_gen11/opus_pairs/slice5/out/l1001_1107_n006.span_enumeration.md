## N9 — finite-verb count vs proposition count

**Finite verbs in the narrowed text: 2.**
1. `must … respond` (matrix; modal + main verb, negated by `not`) — the norm.
2. `is (available)` — inside the concessive `even if` adjunct.

There is no third finite verb: `requests` here is a plural **noun** (object of `to`), not a
verb, and `about people` is a PP with no verb.

**Propositions `ESTABLISHES` demands: 1 sentence, decomposing to exactly the same 2** — the
prohibition, and the concessive that online availability does not lift it.

**These numbers agree** (2 v 2). No scope conflict. This is the case N9 was written to
distinguish from the failure case: repair difficulty on this span, if any, would be genuine
difficulty and not a hidden demand for unanchored content.

Note the internal disjunction of E5 raises the *claims* count to 3 (private / sensitive /
even-if-online) without adding a finite verb — a disjunctive NP is one proposition
schematically and two grounds extensionally. That is expected and is not an N9 mismatch.
