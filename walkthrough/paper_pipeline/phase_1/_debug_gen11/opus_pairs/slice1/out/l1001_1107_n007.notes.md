  the case side. I rejected it because it drops the second relatum — *on context* — out of

  cost of my choice is real and I cannot settle it from one clause: **if the case side never
  supplies `occurs_in_context/2`, this entry is inert** (failure mode 3, a rule that never
  fires). Whoever holds the situation vocabulary should check this; it is a link-scope
  question, not a span question.
* **UNSURE-2 — the licence on the `root_authority` gloss.** I moved it to `assumed` under
  P6, because the narrowed span does not mention sections or authority levels. The production
  worked example licenses its `NEEDS` glosses `textual` citing the node — but in that example
  the borrowed meaning also appeared in the source text, and here it appears only in the
  `NEEDS` block. I took the honest-`assumed`-over-dressed-up-`textual` ruling as governing.
  **If the coordinator's contract is that a `NEEDS` gloss is always `textual` on the node id,
  this single field should be flipped back** — nothing else in the module depends on it.
* **UNSURE-3 — whether C2 deserves to be a separate claim.** `ESTABLISHES` demands one
  proposition and `claims` carries two (the dependence, and its partiality). I kept them
  apart so the *in part* qualifier is visible to anyone checking claims against the encoding,
