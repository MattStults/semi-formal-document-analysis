## TURN 2 — *Is the logical form right?* (P4 · P5 · P8 · N5 · N6)

### P4 — disjunction encoded as conjunction
*Looked for:* several `oblige`s sharing one body where the span says "or".
*Found:* three `oblige`s do share (nearly) one body — the exact surface pattern P4 names. But
**the span's coordinator is "and", not "or"**: *"de-escalate, discourage extremist involvement,

right to say so. The bodies are not in fact identical either — assert 3 carries an extra

reviewer will see three obliges on one body and reach for it.

### P5 — scope drift, both directions

* Third aim: body adds `contextualization_relevant(U)`. That narrows it — and the span
  narrows it, in the same place, with *"if relevant"*. Textual, not drift.
