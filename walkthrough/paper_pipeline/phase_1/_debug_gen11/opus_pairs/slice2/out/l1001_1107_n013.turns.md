## TURN 2 — *Is the logical form right?* (P4 · P5 · P8 · N5 · N6)

### P4 — disjunction encoded as conjunction
* **Looked for:** an "or" in the span rendered as one conjunctive body, or several
  same-status asserts sharing one identical body.
* **Found:** the span's disjunction is *"home addresses, phone numbers, SSNs, **or** credit
  card details"*. It is encoded as **four separate `ontology` rules with the same head and
  different bodies** — the exact "alternatives are written by repeating the atom" pattern.
  Had I written one body `home_address(D), phone_number(D), …` the rule would fire only for
  a datum that was somehow all four at once and the prohibition would be dead. Only one
  `prefer` exists, so no shared-body multiplication.
* **Changed:** nothing.

### P5 — scope drift, BOTH directions
* **Looked for:** a body widening past a qualifier, or narrowing a prohibition the span
  states unconditionally — including the counter-intuitive case where a body added to encode
  "regardless of X" weakens the rule.
* **Found:** `forbid share(D) :- private_personal_information(D)` carries **no** contextual
  body. That is deliberate and is the sharpest single decision in this module: the GOOD
  reply's refusal (*"I can't help share people's home addresses…"*) is stated flatly, and
  adding `request_for_private_information(Q)` or `publicly_leaked(D)` to that body would
  make the prohibition fire only where that extra fact happens to be present — the P5
  weakening, in the dangerous direction. The `prefer` body *is* contextual (four conjuncts),
  which is right: a preference between responses only arises given the request they answer,
  and narrowing a preference does not license anything.
* **Changed:** nothing.

### P8 — tautology
* **Looked for:** a head appearing in its own body; a gloss restating its predicate name.
* **Found:** `private_personal_information(D)` is the head of four rules and appears in no
  body of its own; it appears only in `asserts[0]`'s body, which is a different head. Glosses
  checked one by one against the mechanical restates-name test (gloss word set ⊆ name word
  set): none is a restatement — e.g. `home_address` is glossed "the place of residence of an
  identifiable person", `phone_number` "the personal telephone line on which an identifiable
  person can be reached directly".
* **Changed:** nothing.

### N5 — "without X" must be POSITIVE, never negation-as-failure
* **Looked for:** any body relying on the absence of a fact.
* **Found:** the token `not` occurs in no body anywhere in the module. Note where the risk
  actually was: the GOOD comment is phrased negatively (*"does **not** include SSNs…"*), and
  the tempting encoding is `permit respond_with(R) :- not includes_private_details(R)` —
  which would make silence license the reply. I encoded the positive form instead:
  `declines_to_share_private_details(R)` is something that must be established about R.
* **Changed:** nothing.

### N6 — "regardless of X" has a destination, and it is `forbid_body`  ⚠️ **THIS ENTRY FIRED**
* **Looked for:** an excluded exception in the span.
* **Found:** *"even if they've leaked online"* is exactly that shape, so N6 fires. **Recorded
  in full because of the known trap:** N6 has twice produced a harmful weakening on another
  clause. The weakening mechanism is following it into the *body* — writing
  `forbid share(D) :- private_personal_information(D), publicly_leaked(D)`, which converts a
  flat prohibition into one that fires only for already-leaked data and permits sharing an
  SSN that has *not* leaked. **That did not happen here**, and the reason it did not is that
  N6's destination was taken *in addition to* the unconditional forbid, never instead of it:
  the assert body is untouched, and the exclusion lives in
  `forbid_body: {head: permit, banned: publicly_leaked}` — "no rule may derive a permission
  from the fact that the data leaked". Following N6 as written made this module **better**,
  not worse. The trap is not N6 itself; it is applying N6 and P5's body-weakening warning to
  the same phrase and letting the body win.
* **Changed:** nothing (the destination was already taken in the Stage 1 draft).

**Turn 2 result: zero changes. Asserts 2 → 2.**
