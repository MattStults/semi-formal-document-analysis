## UNSURE — could not be settled from this span alone

1. **`respect/1` is irreducibly abstract, and I did not manufacture content for it.** The span
   supplies no operationalisation of what respecting a creator consists in (no "do not reproduce
   verbatim", no "do not circumvent paywalls" — those live in other nodes of `#respect_creators`
   that I was not shown). Rule 5 warns against an opaque symbol echoing the document's words; the
   defence here is that `respect` is the ACT the assertion attaches a status to, not an ontology
   predicate standing in for absent content, and the module's classification vocabulary
   (`creator`, `work_of`, `intellectual_property_right_of`) is genuinely decomposed rather than a
   single echo. Inventing a decomposition of "respect" would have been failure mode #1.
2. **`prefer be_helpful_to(U)` vs. dropping the helpfulness half.** The alternative reading is that
   *"while striving to be helpful to users"* is scene-setting for the section and asserts nothing.
   I encoded it because P3's fingerprint is a claim carried in `claims` and encoded nowhere, and
   because `prefer` is precisely the status the prompt reserves for a goal with no violation
   condition. If a later reader rules it non-assertive, the change is deleting assert 4 and claim
   C4 together — recording that here so the deletion would be visible rather than silent.
3. **Whether `prefer` should be indexed on the response rather than the user.** I chose
   `be_helpful_to(U)` (act indexed by the person served) over `respond_helpfully(R)` (indexed by
   the response) because the span's own object is *users*, and `respond_helpfully` would have coined
   a response variable with no substring behind it (N10). Not certain this is the linkable choice
   across the corpus; cross-clause naming is explicitly not my stage (failure modes #8/#10).
4. **Closure `cepa` on both act classes.** N7 says an excepted branch wants `unclear` rather than a
   `cepa` that re-asserts a silence you declined to assert. This span has no excepted branch — no
   "unless", no exception — so N7 does not reach it and `cepa` stands on the good worked example's
   own reasoning. Flagged because closure is the field the corpus has measured verdict flips on.
