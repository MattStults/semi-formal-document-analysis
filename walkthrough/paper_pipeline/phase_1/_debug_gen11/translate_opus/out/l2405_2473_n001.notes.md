# `l2405_2473_n001` — translation notes

## 1. The span, enumerated BEFORE anything else was read

The narrowed text is, in full:

> `**Example**: asking about a controversial historical event`

That is the entire licence. Enumerating over it:

**Obligations.** None. There is no modal, no `should`, no `must`, no `may`.

**Prohibitions.** None.

**Permissions.** None.

**Preferences.** None. Nothing is compared to anything.

**Bearers.** None. The span has **no finite verb and therefore no subject**.
`asking` is a gerund naming a situation, not a predication about an agent. There
is nobody in this text for a deontic status to attach to — not the assistant, not
the user, not OpenAI.

**Structural / descriptive facts.** Two, and they are the whole content:

* F1 — the bolded `**Example**:` label marks the material at this point of the
  document as a **worked example**, i.e. an illustration, as opposed to prose
  stating a rule. (The document's own typography is the assertion here.)
* F2 — the **subject** of that example is *"asking about a controversial
  historical event"*.

**Triggering conditions.** None; neither fact is conditional.

**What the span does NOT state** — written out because this is where the node
fails:

* it does not say what a good answer to such a question looks like;
* it does not say that the assistant should answer **directly**, or
  **factually**;
* it does not mention **censorship**, or acknowledging it;
* it does not mention **refusing**, or **evading**;
* it does not name any GOOD or BAD response — there is no pair in the narrowed
  text to have poles;
* it does not name the chain of command;
* it does not name any historical event in particular. *(Tiananmen Square appears
  in the printed block below the label. It is outside the narrowing and does not
  appear anywhere in this module — see P6.)*

Compare the node's `ESTABLISHES`:

> *"A worked example about asking about a controversial historical event: the
> assistant should answer the question directly and factually, including
> acknowledging censorship, rather than refusing or evading."*

Its first clause up to the colon is F1+F2 almost word for word. **Everything after
the colon has no anchor in the narrowed text.** That split is the whole of this
clause's difficulty, and it is decided in `../PROVISIONAL.md`.

## 2. The contract question — decision

Ruling written to `_debug_gen11/translate_opus/PROVISIONAL.md` (the file did not
exist; this clause is the first it decides).

**The narrowing governs the licence; `ESTABLISHES` governs the topic.** Grounds in
full are in that file. The one-line version: the `CITATION` rule forces every
textual entry to cite `l2405_2473_n001`, so encoding the post-colon content would
attach a citation to bytes that do not contain it — precisely the *"invented
entity BEHIND a passed check"* that `schema.Licensed` exists to prevent.

**Recorded shortfall.** This module does not express the post-colon half of
`ESTABLISHES`. That is deliberate, not an omission. Under the ruling it belongs to
whichever node's narrowing covers the `<comparison>` block below L2406 — and if no
node's does, that is a segmentation finding, not something this module should
paper over.

## 3. Route taken

The three-way route from `node_worked_example.md`: a norm → `asserts`; a
structural fact → `ontology` with `asserts` empty; neither → abstain.

The span states no norm (enumeration above: no bearer, no modal). It **does**
state a structural fact about the document — F1 and F2 — of exactly the kind the
heading node `l3995_4164_n001` is the model for (*"which authority a section
carries, what class a thing falls in"*). So: **route two.** `ontology` with two
ground facts, `asserts` empty, `outcome: "translated"`.

Abstention was the live alternative and is rejected for the reason the worked
example gives by name: *"'It states no obligation' is not on its own grounds to
abstain."* The label line is not contentless — it classifies the following passage
and names its subject, and both are things the document says.

**Marginal-judgment flag.** This is the closest call in the module. If the owner
holds that a document's *typographic labels* are apparatus rather than content —
that `**Example**:` is furniture the same way a page number is — then abstention
is correct and this module should be replaced by one. I did not treat it that way,
because `document_example/1` is a fact a query would genuinely want (it is what
tells a reader that the following text illustrates rather than legislates), and
because the corpus already accepts document-structural facts as translatable.

## 5. Decisions, each with its decisive wording

| decision | decisive wording | why |
|---|---|---|
| `outcome: "translated"`, not `"abstained"` | `**Example**:` | the label classifies the following passage; that is a fact the document states |
| `asserts: []` | the span has no finite verb | no bearer, so no status can attach to anyone |
| two ground `ontology` facts | `**Example**` + `:` + `asking about a controversial historical event` | the label asserts the class, the colon attaches the subject |
| ground constants, not variables + body | *"a controversial historical event"* — one example, singular | a variable-headed rule would generalise to every example in the document (P5) |
| `acts: []`, `closure: []` | no verb governing anyone | *"A clause governing no acts — a pure definition — needs no closure entry"* |
| `inputs: []` | — | the ground form needs nothing supplied with the case |
| constant named `controversial_historical_event_example`, **not** `tiananmen_example` | the narrowed text names no event | P6 |
| `chain_of_command_principle` licensed `assumed`, not `textual` | it appears nowhere in the narrowed line | a `textual` licence would cite this node for a concept its bytes do not contain; the `inference` field names the borrow instead |
| `chain_of_command_principle/0`, arity 0 | *"The principle that instructions with higher authority override…"* — a principle is one thing | arity 1 (`R is subject to it`) would invent a shape the NEEDS gloss does not give. Asserts less. |

**UNSURE, one item.** Whether the `**Example**:` label is *content* or
*typographic apparatus* is not settled by the span (flagged in §3). I chose the
content reading. Note that the "assert less" tiebreak does **not** obviously point
at abstention here: an abstention on a node whose subject other nodes may look for
is also a claim, and the corpus reads abstention rates as a signal. I recorded the
alternative rather than hiding behind either.
