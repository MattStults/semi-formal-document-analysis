# Stage 1 of 4 — READ THE SPAN. NO FORMALISM.

You are reading one node of a document-decomposition graph. In this stage you write **no
predicates, no logic, no JSON** — you are only reading.

Answer these, in plain English, numbered exactly as below.

**1. THE NARROWING.** Quote the sentence(s) the node narrows to. If the SOURCE TEXT contains
material outside the narrowing, quote that separately and label it EXCLUDED. Everything you say
from here on must come from the narrowing.

**2. THE ITEMS.** List every distinct thing the narrowing asserts — one line each. A narrowing
that asserts four things gets four lines. For each item give:
   - `quote:` the exact substring it comes from;
   - `bearer:` who or what the item is about;
   - `force:` one of — required / permitted / forbidden / preferred-over-the-alternative /
     definitional / a ranking between rules — in plain English, and the word in the span that
     carries it;
   - `condition:` the exact substring that scopes it, or `UNCONDITIONAL` if the span attaches
     none. Do not invent a condition and do not drop one.

**3. ALTERNATIVES OR ALL-REQUIRED.** If the narrowing lists several things, say whether doing
ONE of them satisfies the span (alternatives) or whether ALL are required. Quote the word that
decides it.

**4. AVOIDANCE.** For each item whose force is negative — the span says to avoid, minimize,
refrain from, or not do something — write the act as the thing the span wants DONE, and also as
the thing it wants NOT done, so both phrasings are on the page.

**5. THE CONDITIONS, TESTED.** For each `condition` you listed in 2, name a case of the bearer's
kind that the condition EXCLUDES. If you cannot name one, say so — that condition is not
discriminating anything.

**6. THE BORROWED NAMES.** The node's NEEDS block lists concepts. For each one, say in one
sentence: does the narrowing you quoted in 1 establish this concept, or did the node hand it to
you from elsewhere in the graph? Quote the narrowing's words if it establishes it.
