# Greenfield Archive — Access Rules

Staff must log every retrieval, and must never leave the vault unlocked.

## Clearance {precedence=charter}

The following clearance levels are listed from highest to lowest.
A room may be entered only by holders of a clearance at or above the room's required level.

1. **Curator**
2. **Fellow**
3. **Visitor**

The vault requires Curator clearance. The reading room requires Visitor clearance.

Where a charter rule conflicts with a house rule, the charter rule prevails. House rules are posted at the front desk.

Example: a Fellow may enter the reading room but not the vault.

The archive was founded in 1911.
